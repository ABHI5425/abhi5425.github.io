How I work

Four beliefs about building with AI.

What I've learned from putting LLMs in front of real users and real queues.

i.

Ground it in a source of truth.

A model is only as trustworthy as what it retrieves. Index the documentation your organization actually stands behind, and the answers get accountable.

ii.

Fix problems where they start.

Catching a bad ticket in triage is cleanup. Stopping it at creation removes the work entirely. The best AI features shift work left.

iii.

Pick the metric before the model.

Deflection, invalid-ticket rate, resolution time, CX. If we can't say which number moves, we're not ready to build.

iv.

Keep humans where judgment lives.

Automate the repeatable, and design clear hand-offs for the calls that need a person. Human-in-the-loop is a design choice, not a fallback.
